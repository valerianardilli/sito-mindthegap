Event staffing agency in Naples for congresses, trade fairs and productions

Hostesses, stewards and promoters for the Mostra d'Oltremare, medical congresses, film productions and the Amalfi Coast.

Quote within 24 hours

We have 13 years of experience in the events sector and five years as a structured agency, with more than 800 active professionals. In Naples we work on congresses, trade fairs, corporate events, audiovisual productions and receptions between the bay and the Amalfi Coast.

Naples is one of Italy's capitals of medical and scientific congresses. These are events with thousands of delegates, complex accreditation, parallel sessions and sponsors who expect absolute order: the kind of work where improvisation shows immediately.

The city is also a permanent film set. Productions working here need extras, on-set reception and logistical support, a chapter we follow with the same attention as events.

Frequently asked

What clients ask us in Naples

How much does event staff cost in Naples?

Duration, headcount, languages and the complexity of the service all count. For congresses we quote for the full run. Send us the details for a figure within 24 hours.

Do you have experience with medical congresses?

It is one of our main areas in the city: accreditation, badges, parallel sessions, confidential materials and speaker management.

Do you provide staff for film productions?

Yes. On-location reception, production office support, extras and crowd coordination.

Do you cover the Amalfi Coast and Capri?

Yes, for international weddings, private parties and high-end hospitality events.

How many people do we need for a congress?

It depends on expected delegates, number of rooms and accreditation desks. We propose a realistic headcount and, if needed, visit the venue beforehand.

Do you provide English speaking staff?

Yes, alongside French, Spanish, German and Russian for international congresses.

How far in advance should I book?

For major congresses we suggest at least a month. For smaller events one or two weeks is enough.

Do you handle uniforms?

On request we take care of dress code and uniforms coordinated with your identity.

Is the staff properly employed?

Yes. All collaborators are formally engaged, in full compliance with employment law.

What if someone does not show up?

The coordinator manages the replacement in real time: service continuity is our responsibility.
