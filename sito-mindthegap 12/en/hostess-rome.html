Frequently asked

What clients ask us in Rome

How much does event staff cost in Rome?

It depends on the length of the event, the number of people, the languages required and the type of service. Send us the details and you will receive a tailored, itemised quote within 24 hours.

Do you provide multilingual hostesses?

Yes. We select staff with English, French, Spanish, German, Russian, Arabic and Chinese for international congresses and foreign delegations.

Do you cover medical and scientific congresses?

Yes, it is one of our areas of specialisation. Staff are trained in accreditation handling, confidential materials and speaker management.

How far in advance should I book?

For complex events we suggest a few weeks. We regularly handle last-minute requests thanks to our network of over 800 active professionals.

We are a foreign company with no Italian office. Can you handle everything?

Yes, this is a situation we deal with constantly. We take care of selection, contracts, briefing and coordination: you simply receive the team on site.

Is the staff properly employed and insured?

Yes. All staff are engaged under regular contracts and covered as Italian employment law requires.

Can I choose the staff before the event?

Of course. We send full profiles with photographs and details, and the final selection is always yours.

Do you provide uniforms?

On request we take care of dress code and uniforms coordinated with your brand identity.

Do you work outside Rome?

Yes. We operate across Italy, including Milan, Florence, Venice, Bologna, Naples, Bari, Sardinia and Sicily.

What happens if someone does not show up?

We handle the replacement in real time. Absences are our problem, not yours.
