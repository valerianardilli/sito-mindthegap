Event staffing agency in Sicily, from Taormina to Palermo

Hostesses, stewards and models for festivals, international weddings, film productions and high-end events across the island.

Quote within 24 hours

Behind us are 13 years of experience in events and five years of activity as an agency, with over 800 selected professionals. In Sicily we cover festivals, congresses, destination weddings, film sets and brand events.

Sicily is the fastest growing destination for international weddings and film productions. Historic villas, ancient theatres and family residences host events for hundreds of guests, often in places where logistics are anything but straightforward.

The island is large and travel times are deceptive: Palermo to Taormina is not an hour's drive. We plan movements in advance and, where useful, build local teams on each side of the island to avoid unnecessary travel.

Frequently asked

What clients ask us in Sicily

How much does event staff cost in Sicily?

Days, headcount, languages and distances all matter. On the island travel weighs heavily: we set it out openly in the quote along with any accommodation.

Do you cover the whole island?

Yes: Palermo, Catania, Taormina, Syracuse, Agrigento, Trapani and the smaller islands. Where it makes sense we build local teams on each side.

Do you staff international weddings?

Yes, it is one of the most frequent requests: guest reception, list checking, timing and liaison with planner and caterer.

Do you work with film productions?

Yes. Set access supervision, crew assistance, extras and crowd organisation.

Do you provide staff for festivals?

Yes: press accreditation, room management, guest escorting and assistance throughout the programme.

Which languages do you cover?

English, French, German, Russian and Arabic are the most requested by the island's international clientele.

How far in advance should I book?

In high season we suggest two months, especially for Taormina and summer weddings. Out of season timings are much shorter.

Is the staff from the island?

We favour local profiles to contain costs. At peak times we supplement with mainland teams, handling travel and accommodation ourselves.

Is the staff properly employed?

Yes. All staff are engaged under regular contracts, in line with employment regulations.

What if someone does not show up?

The coordinator activates the replacement immediately, with reserve profiles planned in from the start.
