Florence

Event staffing agency in Florence for Pitti, fashion and congresses

Hostesses, stewards and models for Pitti Immagine, the Fortezza da Basso, historic palaces and receptions in Tuscan villas.

Quote within 24 hours

The agency has been operating for five years and rests on 13 years of experience in event production, with over 800 professionals in our network. In Florence that means staff for the Pitti Immagine salons, fashion events, congresses and receptions in historic residences.

Florence lives on craftsmanship and detail, and expects the same from whoever greets its visitors. In a city where international buyers come to touch fabrics and assess finishes, front-of-house staff cannot simply smile.

The city concentrates its calendar into a few weeks a year. During Pitti, trained staff become scarce very quickly, so we work on advance bookings and keep a reserve for the major salons.

Frequently asked

What clients ask us in Florence

How much does event staff cost in Florence?

It depends on the number of days, the headcount and the languages required. During the salons we usually quote for the full run of the show. Send us the brief and you will have the detail within 24 hours.

Do you staff Pitti Immagine?

Yes, it is our main appointment in the city. Given the concentration of exhibitors, it is worth securing your team at least a month ahead.

Can your staff handle international buyers?

We select profiles with trade fair experience and verified language skills, briefed on your product before the doors open.

Do you handle weddings and receptions in Tuscany?

Yes, including outside the city. We manage arrivals, the guest list, the timing of the day and liaison with planners and caterers.

Which languages do you cover?

English, French, German, Russian, Japanese and Chinese are the most requested at the Florentine salons.

We are a foreign exhibitor. Can you handle everything?

Yes. Selection, contracts, briefing and coordination are all managed by us.

Do you cover the rest of Tuscany?

Yes: Siena, Lucca, Pisa, Forte dei Marmi and countryside venues for private events and weddings.

Do you work during build-up days?

On request we provide staff for set-up and dismantling days as well.

Is the staff properly employed?

Yes. Each person is formally engaged and covered exactly as the law prescribes.

What if someone does not show up?

The coordinator activates the replacement immediately: we keep reserve profiles precisely for peak periods.
