The rationale

Why these nine

Rome

The home of institutional congresses and luxury events: Fiera di Roma, La Nuvola, the Auditorium, Cinecittà and the palaces of the centre.

Milan

Fashion and design in quantities found nowhere else: Fashion Week, Salone del Mobile, Rho, MiCo and the Quadrilatero.

Florence

Pitti Immagine and luxury craftsmanship, between the Fortezza da Basso, the Leopolda and the Chianti villas.

Venice

Biennale, Film Festival and Boat Show, in a city where logistics travel by water and nothing can be improvised.

Bologna

The heart of Italian B2B trade fairs, Cosmoprof, Cersaie, SANA, and the gateway to the Motor Valley.

Naples

One of Italy's capitals of medical congresses, plus a permanent film set and the Amalfi Coast.

Bari

The Fiera del Levante and a region that has become a European destination for weddings and incentives.

Sardinia

A short, intense season across Costa Smeralda, yachting and international weddings, where scarcity of staff is the real constraint.

Sicily

International weddings, festivals and productions, from Taormina to Palermo, on an island where distances deceive.

We also cover Turin, Genoa, Verona, Rimini and the main Italian exhibition and congress venues. If your event is elsewhere, ask anyway: in most cases we can build the team locally.
