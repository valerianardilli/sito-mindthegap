We are an agency born of 13 years of field work in events, active for five years with a network of over 800 professionals. In Bologna we cover the BolognaFiere salons, congresses and Motor Valley appointments.

Bologna is the city of B2B trade fairs: people do not come here to browse, they come to do business. Staff working a stand at Cosmoprof or Cersaie must tell a buyer from a passer-by, handle a contact form and sustain five days at full pace.

We work extensively with foreign exhibitors arriving without a local structure, providing staff already familiar with the exhibition district, the entrances and the build-up schedules.

Frequently asked

What clients ask us in Bologna

How much does a hostess cost for a trade fair in Bologna?

Pricing is usually per day for the duration of the show. Languages, opening hours and whether you need a coordinator all affect the figure. Send us the brief for a detailed answer within 24 hours.

Do you staff Cosmoprof and Cersaie?

Yes, they are among the shows we work most often. Given the concentration of exhibitors, availability for these salons should be secured well in advance.

Can your staff qualify leads?

Yes. We brief the team on your product and on the qualification criteria you set, so the contacts collected are usable by your sales force.

We are a foreign company with no Italian office. Can you handle everything?

This is the situation we face most often. We handle selection, contracts, briefing and coordination: you receive the team at your stand.

Do you provide German or Chinese speaking staff?

Yes. At the Bolognese salons German, English and Chinese are the languages buyers request most.

How many people do we need for our stand?

It depends on floor area, number of meeting points and expected footfall. We propose an honest headcount and, if useful, visit during build-up.

Do you cover Rimini and the coast?

Yes, we regularly work the trade fairs and congresses in Rimini and across Romagna.

Do you work during build-up?

Yes, on request we provide staff for set-up and dismantling days.

Is the staff properly employed?

Yes. Regular contracts for everyone, with classification compliant with current regulations.

What if someone does not show up?

The coordinator steps in with replacements already available on the exhibition grounds. Your stand is never left uncovered.
