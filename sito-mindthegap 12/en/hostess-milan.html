Frequently asked

What clients ask us in Milan

How much does event staff cost in Milan?

Duration, headcount, languages and the type of role all affect the figure. Send us the brief and we will prepare a tailored quote, itemised and with no obligation.

Do you staff Fashion Week and Salone del Mobile?

Yes, they are among our main appointments. During these periods availability runs out early, so it is worth confirming your team well in advance.

Do you provide multilingual staff?

Yes. Milan runs on international visitors: we have profiles with English, French, Spanish, German and other languages for buyers, foreign press and delegations.

We are a foreign exhibitor. Can you handle everything?

Yes. Selection, contracts, briefing and coordination are all on us: you simply receive a trained team at your stand.

How far in advance should I book?

For complex events a few weeks. During Fashion Week and Salone del Mobile demand is extremely high, so the earlier you confirm the better the selection.

Can I choose the staff beforehand?

Yes, that is our standard practice. We send complete profiles and wait for your confirmation before booking anyone.

Do you provide uniforms?

On request we handle dress code and uniforms aligned with your visual identity.

Is the staff properly employed?

Yes. Everyone we send works under a proper contract, in full compliance with employment regulations.

Do you work outside Milan?

Yes, across Italy: Rome, Florence, Venice, Bologna, Naples, Bari, Sardinia and Sicily.

What happens if someone does not show up?

We manage the replacement in real time, with reserve profiles planned in from the start.
