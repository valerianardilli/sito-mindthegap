Venice

Event staffing agency in Venice for the Biennale and the Film Festival

Hostesses, stewards and models for the Biennale, the Lido, the palaces along the Grand Canal and the most demanding logistics in Italy.

Quote within 24 hours

We bring to the lagoon 13 years of experience in event production and five years of operating as an agency, with over 800 selected professionals. In Venice we work on the Biennale, the Film Festival, the Boat Show, congresses and receptions in historic palaces.

Venice does not forgive improvisation. No van pulls up at the entrance, materials arrive by boat, and a fifteen-minute delay at a landing stage ripples through the entire day. Working here requires staff who know the city, not just the job.

The Venetian calendar is built on peaks: when the city fills up, qualified staff disappear. We work on advance bookings and keep a dedicated reserve for the major appointments.

Frequently asked

What clients ask us in Venice

How much does event staff cost in Venice?

In the lagoon water transfers and travel times also count, and we set them out openly in the quote. Send us dates, venue and headcount for a detailed figure within 24 hours.

Do you work during the Biennale and the Film Festival?

Yes, they are our two main appointments in the city. During festivals availability runs out well in advance, so it is worth booking weeks ahead.

Does your staff know the city?

We select profiles who can navigate vaporetti, calli and landing stages. In Venice, finding your way is an operational skill, not a detail.

Do you manage boat transfers?

We align staff schedules with your water transfers and, if needed, handle arrival logistics together with your supplier.

Do you staff weddings and private receptions?

Yes. Guest reception, list management, timing and coordination with caterers for receptions in palaces and lagoon villas.

Do you provide multilingual hostesses?

Yes: English, French, German, Russian, Arabic and Chinese are the most requested here.

Is the staff properly employed?

Yes. Every person is regularly employed and covered as required by Italian labour law.

Do you also cover Mestre and the mainland?

Certainly, alongside the historic centre we cover Mestre, Marghera and the wider province.

How far in advance should I book?

Out of season one or two weeks is enough. During the Biennale, the Film Festival, Carnival and the Boat Show we recommend at least a month.

What if someone does not show up?

We handle it: we keep reserve profiles in the city precisely for this. You need do nothing.
